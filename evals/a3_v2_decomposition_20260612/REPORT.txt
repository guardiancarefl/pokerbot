=== a3 — PER-DEPTH PAIRED EV DECOMPOSITION: v2 (bbnorm) vs CHAMPION (2026-06-12) ===
RESEARCH_MAP item a3. Existing artifacts only; no simulations. Companion: analysis.json.

QUESTION
v2's bbnorm encoder PASSED the depth-invariance probe (median TV ratio 0.004 vs
deployed 1.55-3.16) yet FAILED its gates (killphilmtt -0.198 vs champion -0.174,
both pre-adapter-fix / same-instrument; calibration 2.55sigma). Depth confusion
not EV-binding, or independent regression?

DATA AND METHOD (and the hard limit)
- G2 per-game records (evals/v2_gate_20260611_pergame/g2/, 24 profiles x 2000
  CRN games) pair by seed against the recorded champion baseline
  (evals/sng_baseline_20260610/), seed_mismatch=0 everywhere. They carry ONLY
  terminal aggregates: seed, hands, level_end, n_alive_end, hero_net.
  -> TRUE per-hand depth EV attribution is IMPOSSIBLE from existing artifacts.
- Proxy used instead: terminal avg surviving stack in BB
  = 9000 / n_alive_end / bb(level_end), computed on the BASELINE trajectory
  (fixed recording -> bucket exogenous to candidate). Buckets <=6 / 6-12 /
  12-25 / >25 bb. Caveat: terminal depth = phase where the game was decided,
  not depth of each decision.
- Cross-checks: per-decision behavior (hero_eff_bb is logged) from the floored
  arms of the two A/B runs — v2 ab (seeds 1-24000) vs deployed
  short_stack_floor_ab_hpl5 (seeds 100001+). Seed spaces DISJOINT -> unpaired,
  behavioral only. Plus G3 self-play net by level_end.

RESULT 1 — THE DEFICIT IS NOT SHALLOW; IT IS MID-DEPTH. THE SHALLOW END FLIPPED TO A GAIN.
Pooled paired delta (v2 - champion), 48,000 paired games, by baseline terminal depth:
    <=6bb   +0.130 / game   n= 9852   z = +11.8   <- where champion depth-confusion lives
    6-12bb  -0.036 / game   n=27746   z =  -6.4
    12-25bb -0.060 / game   n= 7649   z =  -4.4
    >25bb   +0.025 / game   n= 2753   z =  +1.0
    overall -0.0026         n=48000   z =  -0.54  (matches gate REPORT.md)
The shallow gain is broad: 15 of 19 profiles with n>=20 at <=6bb are positive
(e.g. minestacker +0.31 z=+7.3, thefixersng +0.22 z=+7.0, sevenv.2 +0.31 z=+6.6,
millennium +0.27 z=+5.3, gushansen +0.23 z=+3.4, lionmtt +0.42 z=+3.1).
The mid-depth losses concentrate in pressure profiles: 6pack 12-25bb -0.147
(z=-5.0), lionmttv.10 12-25bb -0.182 (z=-3.9) and >25bb -0.262 (z=-3.6),
itmstrikea/c 6-12bb ~-0.12 (z~-3.0), wildbillmtt 12-25bb -0.088 (z=-2.1).
The overall z=-0.54 "panel-neutral" verdict hides two opposite-signed,
depth-localized effects.

RESULT 2 — THE KILLPHILMTT HOLE IS DEPTH-FLAT, UNCHANGED AT EVERY DEPTH.
killphilmtt paired delta by bucket: <=6bb -0.040 (z=-0.61), 6-12bb -0.017
(z=-0.41), 12-25bb -0.028 (z=-0.51), >25bb -0.017 (z=-0.22); overall -0.024
(z=-0.86). C3's was -0.041 (z=-1.44) — also flat. A provably depth-cured
encoder moved the hole by nothing, anywhere. The G2 C2 "FAIL" is a failure to
hit the +0.05 improvement bar, not a regression.

RESULT 3 — THE INDEPENDENT REGRESSION IS A BROAD-SPECTRUM STYLE SHIFT.
Per-decision (floored arms, unpaired): v2 folds MORE facing action at EVERY
depth (0-4bb: 0.571 vs deployed 0.388; 4-6bb: 0.636 vs 0.518; 15-25bb: 0.683
vs 0.603) and carries LESS allin mass at 10-25bb (0.095-0.115 vs 0.143-0.144).
Uniform tightening/passivity — not depth-localized. G3 self-play deficit has
the same shape: losses at L3-L7 (mid depth, -0.03..-0.16), positive at L8
(+0.36) — consistent with the paired decomposition, so the 2.55sigma
calibration miss also sits mid-depth, not shallow.

(1) ANSWER — concentration: NO, inverted. v2's EV deficit is mid-depth
(6-25bb, z=-6.4/-4.4); at the <=10bb depths where the champion's depth
confusion lives, v2 GAINS (+0.130/game at <=6bb, z=+11.8, 15/19 profiles).

(2) VERDICT ON THE a3 FORK — both branches, cleanly split by failure mode:
  (i) For the killphilmtt criterion: DEPTH WASN'T EV-BINDING. The hole is
      flat at every depth under a cured representation (z=-0.86), and G1b's
      floor value did not collapse (+0.320 diverged-only, run-best magnitude).
      It is a policy defect (shove-defense), per RESEARCH_MAP b1 / H2.
  (ii) For the rest of the gate failure (panel profile structure, G3
      2.55sigma): INDEPENDENT REGRESSION — broad tightening producing
      significant 6-25bb losses vs pressure profiles, offset by genuine
      shallow gains. Evidence chain: pooled bucket z's, per-profile table,
      unpaired behavior shift, G3-by-level shape (all in analysis.json).
  Not indeterminate: the depth cure DID translate to EV exactly where it
  should, and what failed is attributable elsewhere.

(3) f-PILLAR / ORGAN-TRANSPLANT IMPLICATION: KEEP the bbnorm encoder.
Status upgrade: from "representationally clean" (probe) to "economically
signed at shallow" (+0.130/game at <=6bb, broad). Its failure modes travel
with the run (style regression, killphil hole, G3 seat asymmetry), not the
encoder; G4 already confirmed live-servability. Confound caveat: one run, one
seed (G=12) — the shallow gain could in principle be run-specific.
