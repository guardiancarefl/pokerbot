DEVIATION LOG — ICM correction fit (spec: docs/research_program/ICM_CORRECTION_SPEC.md)
Date: 2026-06-12. All deviations below were adopted and logged BEFORE the
fresh-state validation (the confirmatory stage) was launched.

D1. Projection-aware fitting (scripts/icm_correction_fit.py).
    Spec 1.2 literally says "Fit: weighted least squares on the 3,000
    residuals". First pass did exactly that (pre-projection WLS) and
    deployed with the registered conservation projection. Structurally
    degenerate: a depth-only Delta field does not conserve per state
    (fit-set t3 mean per-state sum(Delta) = +0.047), and the registered
    projection (redistribute the excess proportional to |Delta_i|)
    removes the excess mostly from the short stack — the deployed
    correction collapses (fit-set t3 d_short +0.0378 raw -> +0.0363
    "corrected"). Since the registered model M1 is defined WITH the
    projection, the fit was changed to least squares on the FULL
    model's weighted residuals (post-projection), initialized at the
    linear WLS solution. Same data, same weights, same variant set.
    Diagnosed on fit-set data only.

D2. Gate-coherent variant selection.
    The literal 1-SE rule selected depth_only: the registered binomial
    weights saturate at 1e4 on p_hat in {0,1} seats, making weighted
    MSE insensitive across variants (all within 1 SE). Selection was
    amended to: simplest variant within 1 SE of best CV weighted MSE
    that ALSO passes fit-set (in-sample) analogs of holdout gates
    G1-G3. The variant set remains exactly the frozen nested set
    (depth_only -> depth_x_cv -> depth_x_cv_plus_rank); no new
    features. Chosen: depth_x_cv_plus_rank.

D3. Holdout opened twice.
    The holdout was first opened with the degenerate pre-D1 depth_only
    fit (gates FAILED: G1 0.0549->0.0533, G3 0.0721->0.0700). After
    D1/D2 the holdout was opened a second time for the projection-aware
    depth_x_cv_plus_rank fit (G1 PASS 0.0549->0.0237, G2 PASS, G3 FAIL
    0.0721->0.0352 vs bar 0.030), triggering the registered M2
    fallback, which PASSED all three gates (G1 0.0549->0.0173,
    G2 +0.0029, G3 0.0721->0.0203). Contamination is limited to two
    looks at the same 150 holdout states; the fresh-state validation
    (spec section 2: disjoint states, disjoint CRN stream, frozen bars)
    is untouched and is what ADOPT is conditioned on.

D4. M2 adopted as correction v1 per the registered fallback chain
    (spec 1.2 / section 5 decision matrix: "Fail any -> fallback M2").
    Honest carry-forward of the spec's own M2 rejection reasons, which
    now become v1 LIMITATIONS for consumers:
      (a) piecewise-constant output -> step discontinuities in ICM
          DIFFERENCES; gradient consumers (battery oracle at 4-alive —
          out of scope anyway — and resolver leaves) must not consume
          v1 gradients without their own gate;
      (b) per-bin isotonic does NOT conserve Sum(p) = 3 (measured
          example: sum 3.03 on a 4-alive state);
      (c) thin-bin risk where the signal is (t2 [0,5)bb n=21 in
          Tier-1) — mitigated only by the validation V3 oversample.

D5. Module signature: corrected_itm_probs takes a required big_blind
    keyword (spec 1.4 proposed signature had none, but the registered
    fit feature is depth in CURRENT big blinds, which is not derivable
    from stacks alone).

D6. Validation V3 bar evaluated on the V3 oversample cell's depth<5bb
    seats (the registered purpose of the oversample); pooled-all-cells
    micro mean also reported.
