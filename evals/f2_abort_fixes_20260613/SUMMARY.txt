PART A — F2 ZERO-STACK ALL-IN  (--allin-zero-stack)
========================================================================

What F2 does: a seat showing the literal digit 0 (post-F1 the consensus
zero survives OCR; a busted/vacated seat reads None) while occupied,
un-folded, and with no current bet is promoted to an ALL-IN seat
(allin_seats[i]=True) IFF a pot-arithmetic COMMITMENT test passes
(residual pot net of visible bets covers one ante per candidate on top
of every chips-visible seat). Promoted seats count toward n_alive and
are treated as occupied-and-in-hand by the dealer-validity check; they
stay alive[]=False so the existing busted-mid-hand / anchor machinery
handles them. Flag OFF -> allin_seats all-False -> byte-identical
(manager: 9750/9750 frames, 0 changes, 17 logs —
flag_off_byte_identity_MANAGER.txt).

Unit tests: tests/test_allin_zero_stack.py — 15/15 pass (this run).
Manager's full suite: 41/41 pass.
