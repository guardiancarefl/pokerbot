  src/nlhe/integration/click_target.py, extended (--extended-click-plans)
  realization ladder for raise_to with no typed-raise UI (commit a6e23f8
  lineage). Rung 3 realized the raise intent as CHECK whenever a CHECK
  button was readable. But a UI where hero can CHECK always permits a
  bet — a missing RAISE/BET button there is by definition a scraper
  misread or the site's pre-action checkbox panel, never a real
  check-only spot. Unlike the spec'd CALL rung (call-only facing-all-in
  UI, where raise is genuinely impossible and CALL is the closest legal
  action), the CHECK rung converts a transient read failure into an
  irreversible forfeit of the betting action. The rung was an
  over-extension beyond the 2026-06-11 must-land spec ("raise_to at
