THE RULE IMPLEMENTED (poker-rules bar): the governing invariant is that
the BIG BLIND advances exactly one ACTIVE player per hand; the small
blind and the button DERIVE from it. Consequences handled:
  - dead button: previous SB player busted -> button on the vacated
    seat; SB posted by the previous BB player; BB one active onward.
  - dead SB: previous BB player busted -> live button, SB seat empty,
    NO SB posted (covered by the pre-existing Predicate 1 bb_only
    detection; sb_seat=None plumbing already existed end-to-end).
  - double elimination: dead button AND dead SB; only the BB posts.
The derivation is NOT "SB/BB = next actives clockwise of the button" as
a rule; the scraped frames' POSTED BLINDS are ground truth. Predicate 1
(_detect_blinds_from_bb_post) walks absolute seats from dealer+1 and
validates SB/BB against the observed posts; the H1 fallback was
generalized to the same absolute-seat walk (arithmetically identical
for alive dealers); the unchanged replay + strict invariant gate is the
final correctness bar (OCR-drift dealer-on-empty frames still drop
there).
