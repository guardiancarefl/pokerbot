[8] WHAT THIS SUGGESTS FOR TAU TUNING (observations only — tau=0.10
    is shipped and registered; no change recommended here)
    The floor's mass removal is dominated by ALLIN (7.53 of 16.32 total pruned mass, 46%) and big bets, exactly the commitment-scaled design intent: tau for an action is tau_max*(chips-added/stack), so near-full-commit actions face thresholds near 0.10 while small bets face near-zero thresholds and are almost never pruned.
    Concentration is overwhelmingly preflop (310/412 decisions, 13.51/16.32 = 83% of pruned mass) — partly exposure (most fresh frames are preflop) but the preflop firing rate (78.4%) also exceeds flop (40.3%) and turn (62.1%). Across depth, firing is high everywhere from 6bb up (66-80%) and per-fire mass is mildly DEEPER-shaded in count but shallower-shaded in intensity (mass|fired 0.065 at 6-10bb vs 0.047 at 40+bb); the floor is nearly silent below 6bb (1/11 fired) where the short-stack floor already owns the action space.
    Hero pot-commitment behaves exactly as the commitment-scaled design intends: firing rate falls monotonically as hero gets committed — 77.8% at 0-5% committed, 58.7% at 5-15%, 47.8% at 15-30%, 25.0% at 30%+ — i.e. the floor prunes speculative full-commit tails when hero has little invested and stands down once hero is genuinely pot-committed (where shove mass is legitimately large and clears tau).
    The seq-315 class (ALLIN tails >5%) is 57 spots ([5]), of which only 5 were actually drawn by the live sampler; the gap between firing rate (71.1%) and altered-decision rate (4.37%) is the intended cheap-insurance profile — most fires remove tails the sampler never drew. Per-fire pruned mass is small (mean 0.056, max 0.152) and argmax changes were 0 at every tau TG2 tested, so at tau=0.10 the floor is purely a tail-shaper, well clear of reshaping mainline play.
